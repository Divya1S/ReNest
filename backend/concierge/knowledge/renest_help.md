# ReNest Concierge Knowledge Base

## What ReNest is

ReNest is a campus move-out rescue marketplace. When students move out of dorms, usable items — storage bins, lamps, mini fans, unopened toiletries — usually end up in dumpsters. ReNest lets students post those items in minutes so other students can rescue them for free or at low cost. Everything is campus-scoped: you browse and post within your own campus community, and pickups happen at campus locations like residence hall lobbies.

## Browse and reserve an item

Anyone can browse listings at /browse — filter by category, price (free or low-cost), and search by keyword. To reserve, open a listing and tap "Request Pickup"; you'll need an account. Your request starts as "Requested". The owner then confirms it and can propose up to 5 pickup time slots; you pick the slot that works for you. Once confirmed, the item is held for you and the listing shows as Reserved. If a confirmed reservation isn't completed within 48 hours, it's automatically released so items don't get stuck.

## The handoff PIN

Every confirmed reservation gets a 6-digit handoff PIN, shown to the claimant (the person picking up). At pickup, the claimant reads the PIN to the owner; the owner enters it in the app, which instantly marks the reservation Completed and the listing Picked Up. This confirms the handoff really happened — no PIN, no completion. After the handoff, the claimant can leave quick feedback on how it went.

## Posting a listing

Go to "New Listing" (or the + quick action button). A listing needs a title, category, condition, price type (free or low cost), a pickup zone (like "Maple Hall lobby"), and an "available until" date — after that date the listing expires automatically. You can add multiple photos; the first becomes the cover. If you attach a photo first, ReNest's AI can suggest the title, category, and details for you. Listings are lightly moderated; flagged listings are hidden from browse.

## Room Scan — clear a whole room at once

Room Scan (at /scan) is for move-out crunch time: photograph your room, and AI detection finds sellable items in the photos and turns each into a draft listing. You then triage each draft — keep it (publish as a listing), mark it for donation, or discard it. The Publish Queue shows how many drafts are ready and publishes them in one tap. A scan session tracks status from Setup → Scanning → Clear-Out → Completed as you work through the room.

## Move-out checklist and tasks

Each scan session comes with a move-out task checklist — setup, listing, pickup, and cleanup tasks with optional due dates. Tasks show as To Do or Done, and the session's progress percentage updates as you complete them. The Dashboard shows your active sessions, pending pickups, and what still needs attention before your move-out deadline.

## Pricing rules — can I sell items or charge money?

ReNest supports exactly two price types: Free and Low Cost. Free is the heart of the platform — most rescued items are free. Low Cost lets you charge a small dollar amount for items with real residual value, but ReNest is not a resale market: no market-price selling, no bidding. ReNest takes no fees and processes no payments — any low-cost money exchange is settled in person, in cash or a payment app, at handoff. If you're unsure, price it free — free items get picked up fastest.

## Safety and pickup guidelines

Meet in public campus locations — residence hall lobbies, front desks, or designated pickup zones, never private rooms. Handoffs happen in person; the 6-digit PIN confirms completion, so never share your PIN before you have the item. Don't post prohibited items (weapons, alcohol, perishable food, recalled goods). If something feels off about a listing or user, report it and the campus moderation team will review it.

## Impact tracking

ReNest tracks real sustainability impact: every completed rescue counts the item's weight as waste diverted from landfill and estimates dollars saved for the receiving student. The /impact page shows campus-wide totals — items rescued, pounds diverted, and money saved — computed from completed handoffs, not projections.

## Alerts and saved searches

Turn on alerts to catch items the moment they're posted. A saved search stores your keyword and filters and checks new listings every few minutes; matches arrive as in-app notifications (and push notifications if you've enabled them). Notification preferences — including quiet hours for push — are configurable in Settings.

## Rescue requests — ask for what you need

If you can't find something, post a rescue request describing what you need, the category, where you can pick up, and when you need it by. Students who are moving out see open requests and can match them with items they're about to post. Requests have an urgency level and close automatically once matched or expired.

## Account, verification, and privacy

Accounts are tied to your campus email; some campuses restrict registration to their .edu domains, and you verify your email after signing up. Your profile shows only your display name and campus — never your email or room. ReNest stores only what the marketplace needs (account, listings, reservations, scan data you create) and doesn't sell personal data. The full policy is at /privacy, where you can also learn how to export or delete your data.

## Getting help with the concierge

The concierge (this assistant) can search live listings, check your own listings, reservations, and room-scan progress, and answer how-to questions about ReNest. It only sees your own activity — never other users' private data — and it can't take actions for you: it looks things up and guides you, while reserving, posting, and publishing stay in your hands.
